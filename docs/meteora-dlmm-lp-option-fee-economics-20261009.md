## 2. Recompute pool-pro-rata fee benchmark

Let W0=$10,000; pool USD TVL T=$4,110,808.13; hourly/daily traded volume reference U=$31,134,324.04 per 24h; on-chain base fee f=0.0004; LP share alpha=0.90; and hold horizon H=13.9834 days.

If the same daily volume and TVL persisted and the investor literally held 10000/T of the liquidity **in every bin**, the conditional 14-day base-fee-only estimate is

```
fee_per_day = U * f * alpha * (W0/T) = $27.2656
F_14 = H * fee_per_day = $381.27
APR_proxy = 365 * (U/T) * f * alpha = 99.52%
```

This is **not** the return of a concentrated personal Position. It is a sensitivity benchmark, ignores dynamic fee variation and price-dependent changes of deposited capital, and depends on *future* U/T.

To test uncertainty, introduce `c`, the ratio of a real LP's eventual captured fees to the pool-pro-rata reference. `F_real = c * $381.27`. It is an illustrative capture factor; cannot be inferred from TVL/volume alone. Even c>1 is mathematically possible for a highly active concentrated position. The price-window worst-case *net excess to HODL* is at least `F_real - option_cost - max_positive_residual` under the **idealized fixed-inventory DLMM approximation**, if those cashflows occur over the option's actual maturity:

The premium-only break-even *annualized captured-fee hurdle* is **32.10%** (3 legs) or **46.28%** (7 legs). Premium PLUS worst static residual hurdle is **39.79%** or **50.42% APR**, respectively. Relative to the optimistic pool-pro-rata estimate of 99.52%, the strategy needs **40.0% or 50.7% actual fee capture**, respectively, to cover cost and the modeled worst static residual on the specified price interval.

This is a **stress-scenario deterministic hurdle, not guaranteed net return**. Both `c` and price-dependent fees are uncertain and path-dependent, and the analytic DLMM model uses an idealized full-bin-conversion payoff. Over other prices the residual can differ; LP exit, capital changes, option execution, liquidity composition, slippage, pool dynamic fees, Solana and Deribit settlement basis, taxes and extra margin are omitted.

## 3. Why real PositionV2 APR can be dramatically different

The default PositionV2 has 70 inline liquidity-share entries, extended PositionBinData permits wider positions. With this pool's 4-bps Bin Step:

- 70 consecutive bins cover a factor `1.0004^70`, about **2.84% full price width** (if centered, about **+/-1.4%**, not +/-10%).
- A **+10% / -10%** move corresponds to roughly **239 / 264 bins**, and **+20% / -20%** to roughly **456 / 558 bins**.
- The user-facing example hedge assumes `W0/T` pro-rata ownership of **all ~4,908 populated pool bins**, which is an artificial basket. A real single 70-bin position cannot have that bin allocation.
- The active bin's reserves are only ~$7k, so a concentrated $10k order would substantially change relative local liquidity. The participant's fee share and out-of-range time cannot be approximated by `10000/pool TVL`.

According to [Meteora's official DLMM account reference](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/docs/blob/main/developer-guides/dlmm/program/accounts.mdx), each bin contains `fee_amount_x_per_token_stored` and `fee_amount_y_per_token_stored` fee-growth checkpoints. PositionV2 holds per-bin liquidity shares, fee checkpoints and pending fees. The existing research `bin_snapshots` table preserves amounts and liquidity supply but **does not preserve cumulative fee-per-unit fields**; likewise no real user's PositionV2 was supplied. Therefore the saved database does not allow an exact 14-day realized LP fee calculation.

## 4. What economics should optimize next?

For a **fixed chosen LP position**, fees that are unaffected by the hedge are constant with respect to option quantity. Adding a fee estimate to the previous `premium + lambda * max_error` problem does **not** magically change the optimal strikes; it changes whether the strategy is worthwhile compared with alternatives. To jointly optimize returns, the chosen *Bin allocation* must become a decision variable, coupled to forward fees and risk, and the options should be measured against a terminal price distribution or a robust minimum-profit constraint.

The current numerical optimizer addresses an **insurance cost-vs-worst-error** objective, not maximum forecast profit. A realistic next calculation requires the PositionV2 bin shares plus actual fee-growth measurements, or at minimum several explicit LP allocation scenarios (e.g. centered 35+35 bins, wider 250+250, and the current pool-pro-rata basket), all priced against the SAME option maturity and snapshot. Do not promise positive APY from only current pool U/T.
